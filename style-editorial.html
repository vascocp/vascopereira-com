Philosophy

"Photography is not just a technique.
It's a way of seeing — and a reason to keep looking."

Every shutter click is a decision. Every frame, a commitment. Vasco believes in photography as a practice of presence — slowing down to notice what others miss.

Workshop

Learn to see
before you shoot.

A full-day immersive photography workshop — indoors and outdoors — led by Vasco Pereira. Equal parts theory, street practice, and honest conversation about what makes an image matter.

Full day · 9h–18h Max 8 people All levels Lisbon
Download programme
09:00 – 10:30Indoor

The Art of Seeing

We open with the hardest question in photography: what are you actually trying to say? Through Vasco's own images and references from masters like Cartier-Bresson and Saul Leiter, this session unpacks light, composition, and emotional intent — before a single shutter is pressed.

Discussion: What drew you to photography? What's the image you've always wanted to make?
10:45 – 12:30Outdoor

Street Walk I — The Decisive Frame

First time on the streets with one constraint: no zooming in post. Participants practice finding the frame before pressing the shutter — geometry, light, human presence. Vasco walks alongside, pointing out missed moments and hidden compositions in real time.

Discussion: Group review of the morning's best 3 shots per person. Why did you press the shutter at that exact moment?
12:30 – 13:30Lunch

Lunch & Informal Edit Review

Participants share their morning images informally over lunch. Vasco comments on the edit selection — not just the images, but the instinct behind what people chose to keep and what they discarded.

13:30 – 15:00Indoor

Light, Emotion & Post-processing

A hands-on session exploring how light shapes mood — from harsh midday sun to the softness of window light. We cover Vasco's editing philosophy: minimal intervention, maximum honesty.

Discussion: When does editing enhance a photo — and when does it betray the moment?
15:15 – 17:15Outdoor

Street Walk II — Golden Hour & Portraits

The afternoon light changes everything. This walk focuses on portraits and human connection — approaching strangers, building trust in seconds, and capturing the unguarded moment.

Discussion: How did it feel to photograph a stranger? What did their face tell you that you didn't expect?
17:30 – 18:00Indoor

Final Presentation & Feedback

Each participant presents their one image of the day — the one that surprised them most. Vasco gives individual written feedback for every participant to take home.

Discussion: What did today change in how you see?
